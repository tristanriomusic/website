About

Tristan Rio creates immersive psychedelic guitar journeys, inspired by a lifelong love of listening to music in bed and drifting toward sleep. Missing music that could truly carry him into dreamland he began crafting his own: rich psychedelic soundscapes grounded in strong melodies and raw, emotional guitar solos.

Recently this vision has led to a series of magical, immersive lying-down concerts where music and visuals invite the audience to fully surrender to the experience. Built entirely live, his performances unfold into hypnotic soundscapes that encourage audiences to dream and reconnect.

In between building dreamscapes Tristan has also played in numerous bands, the most recent one being the explosive and thought evoking folk/punk band Cirque Constance.
